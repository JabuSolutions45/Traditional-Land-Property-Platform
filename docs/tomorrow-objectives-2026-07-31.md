# Umeli objectives for 31 July 2026

Time available: 2 hours  
Primary focus: Finalise the Phase 1 location-data plan

## Desired outcome

At the end of the session, Umeli should have a clear and approved specification for using the MDB 2026 wards with the existing national place directory. The session will prepare the next implementation task without attempting to build the full platform or national mapping programme.

## 0:00–0:15 — Confirm scope and decisions

- Review the location and grant-mapping notes.
- Confirm that MDB 2026 wards are the Phase 1 administrative boundary source.
- Confirm that village results are suggestions unless supported by a reliable boundary.
- Confirm that seller and community corrections require review.
- Record any final changes to these principles.

Deliverable: A short list of approved Phase 1 location rules.

## 0:15–0:45 — Define the seller location experience

- Define what happens when a seller supplies GPS coordinates.
- Specify the automatic results: province, district, municipality and ward.
- Specify how suggested villages, settlements and nearby landmarks are shown.
- Define how a seller confirms a village or submits a correction.
- Define what exact coordinates remain private and what approximate location may be public.

Deliverable: A simple seller location workflow and required fields.

## 0:45–1:20 — Define the MDB 2026 data update

- Compare the current workbook’s 2011 wards with the MDB 2026 layer.
- Confirm the new ward fields and source metadata required.
- Decide how historical 2011 mappings will be retained.
- Define how places will be remapped to 2026 wards without pretending that centroid mapping proves a complete village boundary.
- Define quality checks for the national import.

Deliverable: A controlled workbook and database import specification.

## 1:20–1:45 — Define correction and confidence rules

- Agree on correction statuses: submitted, under review, accepted, rejected and disputed.
- Agree on geographic confidence descriptions.
- Define who may submit, review and approve corrections during the pilot.
- Confirm that official data and community contributions remain separate.
- Confirm that every change retains its source, contributor, date and evidence.

Deliverable: A concise correction and data-quality policy.

## 1:45–2:00 — Close and select the next task

- Review unresolved questions.
- Separate MVP work from grant-funded future work.
- Choose one next implementation task.
- Record acceptance criteria and what is out of scope.

Deliverable: One implementation-ready task for the following work session.

## Recommended next implementation task

Update the national location workbook to include the official MDB 2026 ward directory and source metadata while preserving the 2011 historical ward records. Do not remap every place or build the application until the import and quality rules have been approved.

## Out of scope for this two-hour session

- Building authentication or property listings.
- Importing data into a production database.
- Mapping national village boundaries.
- Purchasing GIS equipment.
- Conducting field surveys.
- Selecting grant funders or preparing a complete funding application.
- Treating approximate property outlines as cadastral boundaries.
