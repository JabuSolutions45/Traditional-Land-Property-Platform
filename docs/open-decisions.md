# Open product decisions

The product owner must approve:

- Final business name, logo and visual identity.
- Identity-verification and SMS providers.
- Map provider and hosting region.
- Privacy-retention periods and public location precision.
- Traditional-authority onboarding evidence and delegation rules.
- Legal notices and dispute escalation.
- Payment architecture, trust arrangements and fees.
- Local-language priorities.
- Final pilot villages and participating traditional authorities.

No item in this list is resolved by the Phase 0 foundation.
